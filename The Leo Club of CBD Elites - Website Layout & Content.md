# **Website Layout & Sitemap Architecture**

The layout framework below details the user interface flow, page breakdown, component layout, and navigation hierarchy for the club's web platform.

## **1\. Global Header & Navigation Bar**

> * **Brand Logo:** Leo Club of CBD Elites Official Emblem (linked to Home)  
> * **Parent Branding:** Lions Clubs International Badge / District Alignment  
> * **Primary Navigation Menu:**  
  * Home  
  * About Us  
  * Leadership & Board  
  * Projects & Events  
  * Membership  
  * Gallery  
  * Contact Us  
> * **Call to Action (CTA) Button:** "Join the Club" / "Donate & Support"

## **2\. Page Structural Wireframe**

| Page Name | Key Layout Sections | Primary Purpose   |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Home Page** | 1\. Hero Banner (Tagline \+ CTA) 2\. Mission & L.E.O. Pillars Snapshot 3\. Impact Metrics (Events, Hours, Members) 4\. Featured Service Projects 5\. President's Welcome Message 6\. Latest News & Upcoming Events | Primary landing page to engage visitors, showcase impact, and direct potential members. |
| **About Us** | 1\. About Leo Clubs & L.E.O. Motto 2\. History of CBD Elites 3\. Lions Clubs International Affiliation 4\. Core Values & Vision Statement | Establishes organizational credibility, heritage, and alignment with Lions District goals. |
| **Leadership & Board** | 1\. Executive Officers Grid (President, Secretary, Treasurer) 2\. Directors & Chairpersons Grid 3\. Lions Club Advisors & Mentors 4\. Past Presidents Honor Roll | Highlights active club leadership and recognizes governance structure. |
| **Projects & Events** | 1\. Category Filters (Community, Environment, Youth, Health) 2\. Ongoing & Upcoming Campaigns 3\. Past Projects Archive & Impact Cards | Showcases community service initiatives and invites event participation. |
| **Membership** | 1\. Why Join Leo Club CBD Elites? 2\. Member Benefits & Growth Pathways 3\. Membership Eligibility & Steps 4\. Embedded Join Request Form | Drives youth recruitment and membership application conversions. |
| **Contact Us** | 1\. Direct Inquiry Form 2\. Meeting Location & Map Coordinates 3\. Social Media Hub Links 4\. Official Email & Phone Contacts | Facilitates direct communication for partners, sponsors, and prospective members. |

### **Who We Are**

The Leo Club of CBD Elites is a vibrant youth organization chartered under **Lions International, District 3231 A4 — Leo Multiple 3231**. Based in Navi Mumbai, our club brings together young leaders dedicated to creating measurable impact through service, compassion, and fellowship.

### **The L.E.O. Motto**

* **Leadership:** Developing skills as project organizers, time managers, and team leaders.  
* **Experience:** Learning how teamwork and dedication can solve real-world community challenges.  
* **Opportunity:** Discovering the satisfaction of volunteer service and building lifelong connections.

### **Our Pillars of Impact**

1. **Child Welfare:** Uplifting and empowering children through learning, creative workshops, and everyday kindness.  
2. **Animal Welfare:** Protecting, feeding, and caring for stray animals across our city.  
3. **Youth & Leadership Development:** Nurturing capable young leaders who lead by serving.  
4. **Community & Inclusion:** Fostering fellowship, dignity, and a sense of belonging for all sections of society.
